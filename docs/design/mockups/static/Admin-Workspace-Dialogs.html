Delete a workspace

Opened from Delete on a workspace row.

Delete Support?

This cannot be undone. Deleting Support removes:

  • Its conversations and request traces
  • Its API keys, which stop working at once
  • Its routes, tools and provider keys

Its 6 people keep their accounts. Its spend stays in the instance report.
