Pattern 4

Error alert and notice

HeroUI: Alert. Errors are danger with role alert. Notices are neutral: grey fill, no border, an info icon, role status in chat and role note on a page. One plain sentence on what happened, one on what to do. Never a status code.

Pattern 11

Field with a unit

HeroUI: InputGroup with a text prefix or suffix. The unit is plain muted text inside the field, with no fill and no divider, so the group reads as one input. The focus ring goes round the whole group. The currency is in the label for screen readers and the $ is aria-hidden. A suffix unit is linked with aria-describedby.

Pattern 12

Settings section

HeroUI: Card, Checkbox, Select, InputGroup, Button. One option per row: label and description on the left, the control on the right, a divider between rows, Save under the last divider. On/off options are checkboxes, because nothing applies until Save. A Switch is only for a change that applies at once, like Asks first in a tool list.

Pattern 13

Form dialog

HeroUI: Modal. Every add, edit and replace action opens one, and the page underneath stays a list. Header with the title and a close button, a divider, the body, a divider, the footer. Buttons sit on the right with the primary last. A destructive action sits bottom left as a danger text button. One-time secret dialogs have no close button (Pattern 5).

Pattern 14

Card header, inner table and paging

HeroUI: Card, Table, Button. A card's note goes under its title as a subtitle, never beside it. A table next to other content gets a border and rounded corners. Paging sits under the card, outside it: the count on the left, Previous and Next on the right, Previous disabled on the first page with aria-disabled. Lists sorted by time say Newer and Older. A short preview ends with a Show all link and no pager.

Pattern 15

Tabs and pills

HeroUI: Tabs, in two styles. Underline tabs switch between sections of a page. Pills switch the view inside one card. Both are a tablist: arrow keys move between tabs, and the panel is labelled by the selected tab.
